## Robust DAgger evaluation

Both policies were tested on the same 100 trials with 2.5x pose randomization, object mass and friction variation, 1.5 mm observation noise, and two control steps of action delay.

| Measurement | Behavior cloning | DAgger |
|---|---:|---:|
| Success rate | 51% | 82% |
| Mean final error | 14.43 mm | 6.35 mm |
| 95th-percentile final error | 38.12 mm | 16.77 mm |

Two DAgger rounds were selected. A third round achieved 73% and was rejected using closed-loop evaluation; its report remains in `artifacts/dagger/ablation_three_rounds.json`.
